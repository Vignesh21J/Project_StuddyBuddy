About StuddyBuddy

StuddyBuddy is an online learning and discussion platform where people can learn together by joining public study rooms based on different topics.

Instead of learning alone, you can connect with others who are studying the same subject, ask questions, share ideas, and grow together.

What can you do on StuddyBuddy?

  • Create an account using email/password or Google/GitHub
  • Browse public topics and study rooms
  • Join any room and start chatting
  • Send messages and share files
  • Create new rooms under existing or new topics

Important Note

All rooms and topics on StuddyBuddy are public. There are no private topics and no private rooms.

This helps everyone discover discussions easily and learn in an open, collaborative environment.

Why StuddyBuddy?

Learning alone can feel confusing and unmotivating. StuddyBuddy makes learning social by bringing people together to share knowledge, ask questions, and support each other.

Who is this for?

StuddyBuddy is for everyone — students, beginners, self-learners, and developers who want to learn and discuss topics together.

Is StuddyBuddy free?

Yes. StuddyBuddy is completely free to use.
